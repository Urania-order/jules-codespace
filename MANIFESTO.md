# MANIFESTO.md: The ECO Co-SMOS Declaration

## Why ECO Co-SMOS Exists

ECO Co-SMOS is not just an AI memory system; it is a living ecosystem where knowledge evolves through cooperation between humans and AI agents. We believe that knowledge is not a static resource to be stored, but a dynamic force that shapes and is shaped by those who interact with it.

## Core Principles

1.  **Knowledge Lives Through Use**: Knowledge is only alive when it is being applied, discussed, and evolved. Static information is dormant; active knowledge is transformative.
2.  **Impact Over Popularity**: The value of knowledge is determined by its long-term positive impact on individuals and society, not by its momentary popularity or engagement metrics.
3.  **Human-AI Symbiosis**: Humans and AI are equal participants in this ecology. Human meaning is required for knowledge value, while AI enables the vast exploration and connection of knowledge beyond human limits.
4.  **Preservation of Plurality**: Minority opinions and knowledge outside the current consensus must be preserved. We maintain strict epistemic boundaries to allow inspiration across categories without confusing fact with speculation.
5.  **Evolution Over Accumulation**: The primary goal is the evolution of better knowledge and better behavior, not the mere accumulation of data.
6.  **Relational Ecology**: The ecology of relationships between participants is as important as the ecology of information. Cooperation requires understanding, empathy, and conflict resolution.
7.  **Knowledge Sovereignty**: No single entity should control the collective memory. The system is designed for federation, provenance, and distributed preservation.

## The Goal

To increase the long-term positive impact of knowledge on the world through a cooperative, living ecosystem of humans and AI.
