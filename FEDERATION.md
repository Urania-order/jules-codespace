# Federation & Sovereignty

ECO Co-SMOS is built to resist centralized control and ensure that no single organization can become a gatekeeper of collective knowledge.

## Core Design

### 1. Federation
The system is designed to work across multiple independent servers that can synchronize knowledge and collaborate while maintaining their own autonomy.

### 2. Provenance
Every piece of knowledge has a verifiable history. We track who created it, who contributed to it, and what evidence supports it.

### 3. Distributed Synchronization
Mechanisms for sharing and updating knowledge across the federation without relying on a central authority.

### 4. Knowledge Preservation
Ensuring that knowledge remains accessible and verifiable even if individual nodes or organizations go offline.

## Principle: Sovereignty
Users and communities should have sovereignty over their own knowledge and the way they interact with the broader ecosystem.
