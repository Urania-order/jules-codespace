# Research Portals

Research Portals are the primary mechanism for funding and driving the evolution of knowledge in ECO Co-SMOS.

## Framework

- **Collective Sponsorship**: Multiple participants can contribute funds or resources to a research project.
- **Transparent Budgets**: All funding and expenditures are tracked and visible to the community.
- **Community Participation**: Research is not done in isolation; the community can participate in setting milestones and reviewing outcomes.
- **Experimental Milestones**: Funding is often tied to the achievement of specific, verifiable experimental goals.
- **Public Outcomes**: The results of funded research are integrated back into the knowledge ecology for the benefit of all.

## Neutrality
The architecture is designed to be neutral regarding payment providers or underlying technologies (e.g., blockchain). It provides the framework for tracking and coordination, while integration with specific financial systems is handled via modular adapters.
