# Knowledge Ecology

Knowledge in ECO Co-SMOS is not just stored data; it is part of a living ecosystem.

## Key Processes

### 1. Lifecycle Management
Knowledge moves through states from a raw idea to collective experience and improved versions.

### 2. Propagation & Diffusion
How knowledge spreads across different clusters and reaches different participants (humans and AI).

### 3. Resonance
Delayed and indirect effects of knowledge. A discovery in one domain may "resonate" and trigger a breakthrough in another, even if the connection isn't immediately obvious.

### 4. Activation & Dormancy
Knowledge that is frequently used and applied is "active." Knowledge that loses its practical application becomes "dormant," but remains available for future "reconstruction."

### 5. Evolution & Reconstruction
Knowledge is continuously refined. Even forgotten or lost knowledge can be reconstructed from artifacts and observed outcomes (Backward Discovery).
