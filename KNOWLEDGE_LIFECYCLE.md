# Knowledge Lifecycle

Every knowledge object (MemoryNode) in ECO Co-SMOS traverses a lifecycle. Knowledge never reaches a final, static state.

## Lifecycle States

1.  **Idea**: A raw thought, observation, or spark of inspiration.
2.  **Discussion**: An idea that is being debated, refined, and contextualized by humans and AI.
3.  **Recipe**: A structured process or method derived from the discussion.
4.  **Experiment**: The application of a recipe in a specific context to test its validity.
5.  **Practice**: A successful experiment that has become a regular activity or habit.
6.  **Behavior Change**: Documentation of how the practice has altered the behavior of participants or the system.
7.  **Collective Experience**: The aggregation of results from many participants practicing the same knowledge.
8.  **Improved Knowledge**: The refinement of the original idea/recipe based on the collective experience, starting a new cycle.

## Transition Triggers
Transitions are triggered by evidence: successful executions, user feedback, causal analysis, and consensus reaching.
